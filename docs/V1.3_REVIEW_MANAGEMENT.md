# V1.3 Phase 9 — Forward Review & Management Reporting

## Scope

Phase 9 evaluates already-recorded research decisions against later **confirmed NAV only**. It is not a backtest center and it never rewrites historical decisions. The output is observational: decision reviews, AI role scorecards, and weekly/monthly management reports.

## Forward review lifecycle

`ResearchInboxItem(PROCESSED/BLOCKED)` -> `DecisionReview(PENDING)` -> target date -> later `NavConfirm(confirmed=true)` -> `RESOLVED`.

Default horizons are 5 / 20 / 60 calendar days and can be changed with `DECISION_REVIEW_HORIZONS_DAYS`. Each `(research_item_id, horizon_days)` pair is unique.

Reference and target prices come only from `NavConfirm`. ESTIMATED / PROVISIONAL / PENDING NAV never resolves a review. BUY is directionally correct when the forward return is positive; SELL is correct when it is negative. Confidence calibration uses squared error against the directional outcome.

Execution outcome is tracked independently from decision quality: `EXECUTED`, `RISK_BLOCKED`, `USER_CANCELLED`, `EXPIRED`, `EXECUTION_FAILED`, `PENDING_OR_NOT_EXECUTED`, and related non-trade states. This avoids rewarding or punishing a model for an order outcome that never actually executed.

## AI employee scorecards

The scorecard is transparent and role-specific:

- Kimi / research: call success + evidence traceability.
- Qwen / structure: call success + evidence traceability.
- DeepSeek / CIO: call success + evidence traceability + resolved directional hit rate + confidence calibration.
- Cost is reported but is not used as a reward signal.

Unavailable components are omitted and the remaining score weights are renormalized. A scorecard is **not** permission to auto-tune a prompt, change a strategy, change risk limits, reroute the CIO, or alter an order.

## Management reports

Weekly and monthly reports use formal exact-NAV `PortfolioSnapshot` rows for performance. They also summarize decision-review maturity, execution outcomes, order statuses, risk rejects, operational alerts, reconciliation states/diffs, AI estimated cost, and AI role scorecards.

A report is `FINAL` only when the period-end formal snapshot exists and all reviews that should have matured by the period end are resolved. Otherwise it remains `PROVISIONAL`; later runs may create a higher revision. Historical revisions are append-only.

## Scheduler

All times use configured `TIMEZONE` (default Asia/Shanghai):

- 23:35 Mon-Fri: sync newly persisted decisions into review rows and resolve due reviews from confirmed NAV.
- 23:50 Mon-Fri: if the day is the last CN trading day of the week and/or month, generate due weekly/monthly reports.
- Same-local-day report retries are idempotent; a later day may create a revision if a prior report was provisional.

## API

Read endpoints are authenticated. Refresh/generation operations require system or ADMIN:

- `GET /reviews/decisions`
- `POST /reviews/sync`
- `POST /reviews/resolve`
- `GET /reviews/ai-performance`
- `GET /management/reports`
- `POST /management/reports/generate`

## Hard boundaries

1. No standalone historical backtest center is introduced.
2. No future NAV, estimated NAV, or stale valuation is promoted to a formal outcome.
3. AI scorecards do not mutate prompts, risk rules, strategies, model routing, or orders.
4. Phase 9 remains personal research + forward simulation only. Live trading stays disabled.
